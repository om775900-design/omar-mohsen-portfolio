# 3 practice projects for your portfolio

These are self-initiated demo projects. Label them as personal/concept work, not client work.

## 1. Product Reel — 15 seconds
**Goal:** Make an everyday product look premium.
- Find footage you recorded yourself, or use properly licensed stock footage.
- First 1–2 seconds: strongest close-up or movement.
- Middle: 3–5 short detail shots cut to music.
- End: a clean product shot and a simple call-to-action.
- Premiere Pro skills: trimming, keyframes, speed changes only where appropriate, sound effects, basic color correction.
- Export: vertical 1080 × 1920, H.264 MP4.

## 2. Fitness Motivation — 20 seconds
**Goal:** Build energy and rhythm.
- Use your own workout footage or properly licensed clips.
- Start with an action shot, not a logo.
- Cut to the beat; use short, readable captions.
- Keep music and effects balanced; avoid clipping.
- Export: vertical 1080 × 1920, H.264 MP4.

## 3. Educational Reel — 30 seconds
**Goal:** Make a simple tip easy to follow.
- Write a 3-point script with a strong opening question.
- Use jump cuts to remove pauses.
- Add accurate captions with consistent font, size, and margins.
- Emphasize only a few important words.
- End with a concise summary or call-to-action.
- Export: vertical 1080 × 1920, H.264 MP4.

## Suggested learning order
1. Make the 15-second product edit first.
2. Make the fitness edit to practice beat sync.
3. Make the educational edit to practice pacing and captions.
4. Watch each export on your phone, fix issues, then replace the matching website concept with the finished video.
5. Aim for 3 strong pieces rather than 10 unfinished ones.

## Copyright reminder
Use footage and music you own or have permission/licensing to use. Don't download and re-upload another editor's work as your own.
